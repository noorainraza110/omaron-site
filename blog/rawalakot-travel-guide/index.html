Rawalakot Travel Guide: 7 Best Places to Visit in the Pearl Valley (2026)

Most travelers searching for the best places to visit in Azad Kashmir often overlook the "Pearl Valley." Located in the Poonch District, Rawalakot is a rare high-altitude escape that's actually accessible. At 5,374 feet, it's the perfect base for exploring the Pir Panjal Range.

Published March 25, 20267 min readBy OmarOne

Whether you're looking for a honeymoon cottage in Rawalakot or a quiet spot for a mountain workation, this guide covers the must-sees and the hidden corners.

1. Toli Peer: The Roof of Poonch District

If you want to scale the sky, head to Toli Peer. It's the highest point in the region.

SEO Insight: People often search for "Toli Peer trekking guide."

The Experience: On a clear day, you can see all the way to Nanga Parbat. It's a dizzying, beautiful spot that makes most northern area hotels feel like they're in a basement by comparison.

2. Banjosa Lake: Escape the Crowds

As the most famous tourist attraction in Rawalakot, Banjosa Lake can get busy. It's an artificial lake surrounded by dense pine forests that looks like it belongs in the Swiss Alps.

The Local Hack: Avoid the main jetty. Hike the trails on the far side of the lake for better photography and peace. This is the prime spot for those looking for lakeview hotels in Rawalakot.

The Workation Factor: We know you need to stay connected. While most of the valley has spotty signals, we've prioritized high-speed internet and power backup, making us the top choice for remote work in the mountains.

4. Ghori Mar & Devi Gali: The Hidden High Meadows

While everyone flocks to the lake, the real Pearl Valley magic is in the meadows of Ghori Mar. These rolling grasslands are perfect for summer camping or a quiet afternoon of meditation. It's one of those hidden gems in Kashmir that hasn't been spoiled by over-tourism yet.

5. Nergola Waterfall: A Refreshing Detour

Just a short drive from the main city, Nergola Waterfall is a great spot for a quick dip or a picnic. It's one of the best things to do in Rawalakot if you have a spare afternoon and want to hear the sound of rushing water instead of traffic.

Expert Travel Tips for Your Rawalakot Trip

Best Time to Visit: For the greenery, visit between April and June. For the Rawalakot snow season, aim for January or February.

Distance from Islamabad: It's a scenic 3.5-hour drive (approx. 115 km) via the Gulyana Road or the Kahuta route.

What to Pack: Even in the height of summer, the weather in Rawalakot at night can be chilly. Bring a light fleece.
